07. Special Showcase

7.1 Special Sequence (Exercise Scenario)

This module provides pre-configured scenarios for professional demonstrations, specifically designed for Air Force Chief presentations. These scenarios require a specially trained operator and precise timing for perfect synchronization.

How to Load the Scenario

To begin, navigate to the bottom-left Option Menu and click the Load Sequence button. This action will automatically clear all existing aircraft from the scene and instantly spawn a new set of scenario-ready units. Once loaded, you can manage these aircraft through arrival and departure stations just like standard aircraft control.

Sequence Calls

Within the same Option Menu, clicking Sequence Call or (Ctrl+W) opens a specialized window containing additional voice commands tailored for these scenarios. You can play these audio clips instantly by clicking any item from the pilot call list, functioning exactly like the standard pilot call buttons found in the main command node.

7.2 Airport Vehicles & Ground Operations

This module lets you control three essential ground vehicles at VGHS airport: Inspection, Dragchute, and CAAB vehicles. You can easily activate, pause, or resume any of these at any time using the following keyboard shortcuts.

Keyboard Shortcuts & Controls

  • Inspection Vehicle: Press I or Numpad 1 to Activate & then Pause/Resume.
  • Dragchute Vehicle: Press D or Numpad 2 to Activate & then Pause/Resume.
  • CAAB Vehicle: Press C or Numpad 3 to Activate & then Pause/Resume.
Note: Quickly pressing the assigned key (e.g. I or D or C) multiple times will Delete/Remove the vehicle from the scene.
