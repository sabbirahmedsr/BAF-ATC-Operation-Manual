08. VFX & Fun

This module adds visual effects and interactive elements to your simulation, enhancing the atmosphere for various events and presentations.

Flypass Demonstration

The Flypass effect is designed to provide a grand welcome for the incoming guests or high-profile visitors. You can trigger this at any time by pressing the Space button while the simulation is running. Currently, this feature is only available for VGHS.

Animations & Visuals Shortcut

  • Acrobatic Show: Press Space to trigger the acrobatic animation.
  • Formation Flight: Press Numpad 0 to initiate the formation flight sequence.
  • Fireworks: Press F to launch fireworks for special celebratory events.
Note: The Formation Flight is essential for our Special Showcase sequence too. We begin our special showcase by playing this animation. Alternatively, you can trigger this animation directly from the Sequence Call UI window by clicking the Play Anim button located at the bottom-left corner of it.

More VFX Keyboard Shortcut

  • Thank You Message: Press T to toggle the thank you text animation.
  • Bad Weather Warning: Press W to display the severe weather alert.
  • Night Operation Alert: Press N to display the night operation notification.
  • Barrier Control: Press B to toggle the movement (Up/Down) of the security barriers.
