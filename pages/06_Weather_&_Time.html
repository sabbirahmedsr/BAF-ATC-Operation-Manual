06. Weather & Time

The ATC Simulator integrates a highly sophisticated, procedural sky and weather system. This architecture enables real-time atmospheric manipulation, delivering a deeply immersive environment. The following illustration depicts a typical foggy day, which creates an exceptaional challenging situation for an ATC with low visibility, demonstrating the system's dynamic rendering capabilities.

To access the environmental controls, navigate to the Sky and Weather panel via the options menu (located at the bottom-left corner of the interface, indicated by a list icon), or simply utilize the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + W.

Note: If you find the extensive settings overwhelming, it is always recommended to select a pre-configured weather profile for a quick and consistent setup.

6.1 Weather Profiles (Presets)

For rapid configuration, the simulator includes ten pre-configured weather profiles. You can easily select these from the "Weather Profile" dropdown menu, located at the top section of the Sky and Weather control popup. A short description of each is as follows:

  • Default: Standard atmospheric conditions for routine training.
  • Sunny Day: Clear skies with optimal visibility.
  • Cloudy Day: Partially overcast skies with light cloud coverage.
  • Overcast: Dense cloud coverage reducing overall brightness.
  • Rainy Day: Moderate rainfall with associated atmospheric effects.
  • Storm: Intense weather with heavy rain, low visibility, and lightning.
  • Dark Night: Standard night environment with minimal ambient light.
  • Moonlit Night: Night operation with significant illumination from the moon.
  • Foggy: Low visibility conditions simulating ground-level mist.
  • Rainbow: A specialized post-rain visual effect.
  • Aurora: An experimental atmospheric effect for visual variety.

6.2 Custom Settings (Advanced)

For advanced users requiring specific training environments, the system offers granular control over time, location, and atmospheric parameters. These settings are categorized within the Sky and Weather control interface.

Time and Location

This module allows for precise calibration of the simulation's temporal and geographical state. Specially you can set Day/Night mode from here using Hour of day slider.

  • Month of Year: Sets the calendar month to adjust seasonal lighting and environment.
  • Day of Month: Defines the specific day to adjust sun positioning and lunar cycles.
  • Hour of Day: Manually selects the time to simulate dawn, daylight, dusk, or night.
  • Auto Change Speed: Controls the progression rate of the dynamic time system.
  • Latitude & Longitude: Sets the precise geographical coordinates of the airport.
  • Orientation: Rotates the global celestial direction to match runway headings.
Tip: Use the Auto Change Speed slider to create dynamic, time-lapse scenarios that test a trainee's adaptability to changing light conditions.

Cloud Setting

This section provides comprehensive control over cloud formations, density, and movement to realistically simulate various sky conditions.

Basic Cloud
  • Alto Stratus Amount: Adjusts the density and coverage of mid-level stratus clouds.
  • Cirrus Amount: Controls the visibility and spread of high-altitude wispy clouds.
  • Cumulus Amount: Defines the quantity and prominence of puffy, low-level clouds.
  • Cloud Scale: Sets the overall size and uniformity of the cloud formations.
Cumulus Parameter
  • Quality: Determines the rendering fidelity and sharpness of cumulus clouds.
  • Details: Adjusts the structural complexity and graininess of cloud textures.
  • Frequency: Sets the occurrence rate of cloud patches across the sky.
  • Speed: Controls the velocity at which clouds drift across the horizon.
  • Direction: Defines the movement heading of the clouds to match wind flow.

The following visualization demonstrates the system's dynamic rendering capabilities, showcasing a realistic cloudscape achieved through precise adjustments of the Basic Cloud and Cumulus Parameter settings.

Weather Setting

This module provides control over atmospheric conditions, allowing for the simulation of diverse meteorological scenarios to challenge ATC operations.

  • Fog Density: Adjusts the thickness of ground-level mist to limit visual range.
  • Humidity: Sets the moisture level in the air affecting atmospheric clarity.
  • Temperature: Defines the ambient thermal state, influencing overall weather behavior.
  • Overcast Amount: Sets the intensity of cloud cover obscuring direct sunlight.
  • Rain Amount: Controls the volume and intensity of rainfall.
  • Lightning: Adjusts the frequency and intensity of lightning strikes during storms.
  • Snow Amount: Sets the volume and accumulation intensity of snowfall.
  • Rainbow: Toggles the intensity of the rainbow effect post-precipitation.
  • Wind Amount: Controls the velocity and strength of wind gusts across the airfield.
Note: Some parameters may overlap on other parameter. For example, setting Overcast Amount to its maximum will fully cover the sky, so you can not see any changes made on Cumulus or Cirrus setting.

Sound Setting

This section allows users to balance the audio output of the simulation, ensuring optimal clarity for training exercises as shown in image_16377d.png.

  • Master Volume: Adjusts the global audio output level for the entire simulation.
  • Wind Volume: Controls the intensity of ambient wind audio effects.
  • Day Volume: Sets the background sound levels for daytime environment presets.
  • Night Volume: Defines the volume level for nighttime ambient soundscapes.
  • Rain Volume: Adjusts the audio prominence of precipitation effects.
  • Thunder Volume: Controls the impact and volume of storm-related thunder sounds.

More Parameter

This section provides advanced lighting adjustments to fine-tune the visual environment.

  • Day Sky Light: Adjusts the brightness of the sky during daytime.
  • Day Surface Light: Controls the intensity of ground surface light during the day.
  • Night Sky Light: Modifies the luminosity of the sky during nighttime.
  • Night Surface Light: Regulates the brightness of ground surfaces during night.

6.3 Additional Controls

At the bottom of the popup, additional utility functions are available to manage the environment:

  • Light : ON/OFF: Toggles the simulation's artificial lighting systems.
  • Random: Generates a randomized weather profile for unpredictable training scenarios.
  • Reset All: Reverts all atmospheric settings to the system default.
  • Back: Closes the weather popup and returns to the previous menu.
Note: Since the system is procedural and runtime-based, changes made to custom settings will take effect immediately without requiring a restart of the simulation.

6.4 Day/Night Operation

Users can manage time and lighting conditions through two primary methods:

  • Weather Presets: Quickly select scenarios like Dark Night or Moonlit Night for instant environmental changes. For twist, Stormy Day also make the light as dark as night.
  • Custom Settings (Advanced): Use the Time and Location module in the Sky and Weather interface, where the Hour of Day slider allows manual transitions between dawn, daylight, dusk, and night cycles.
